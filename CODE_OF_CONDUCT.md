# Code of Conduct

MtaaFix is a community platform and project. We are committed to providing a
welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful and inclusive.
- Accept constructive criticism.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Show empathy towards other contributors.

## Enforcement
Report violations to the project maintainers. Severe violations may result in
temporary or permanent removal from the project.
